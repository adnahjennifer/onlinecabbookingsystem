Driver ride lifecycle guide

Day 4 Lifecycle Workflow
  1. 1.
    Receive request

    Passenger books a cab. Request displays pickup, destination, vehicle category, and estimated fare.

  2. 2.
    Accept or reject

    Accepting reserves the booking, marks driver as busy, and advances status to Accepted.

  3. 3.
    Start trip

    When arriving at the passenger's pickup location, click Start Trip to begin the active ride.

  4. 4.
    Complete trip & record fare

    Upon reaching the destination, complete the trip. The final fare is committed to earnings and trip history.
